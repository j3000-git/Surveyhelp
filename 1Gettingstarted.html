Create your SurveyTaps account

Overview

Your SurveyTaps account gives you a place to create and manage your surveys. You can return to your dashboard to finish a draft or check your results.

Prerequisites

  • An email address you can access.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Open SurveyTaps and click Sign up.
  2. Enter the details requested on the sign-up form.
  3. Click Create account.

[placeholder]

  1. If SurveyTaps asks you to verify your email, open the verification message and click Verify email.
  2. If prompted, click Sign in and use the same email address to open your dashboard.

Tips & Troubleshooting

  • If the verification email doesn’t arrive, check your spam folder and make sure you entered the correct email address.
  • If you already have an account, click Sign in. Use Forgot password? if you need to reset your password.

Find your way around the dashboard

Overview

The dashboard is where you start a survey or return to one you’ve already created. Use it to find your work and open the survey you want to edit or review.

Prerequisites

  • A SurveyTaps account.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Click Sign in to open your dashboard. If you’re already signed in and on another page, click Dashboard.
  2. Find your survey list and look for the title of the survey you want to work on.

[placeholder]

  1. Click the survey title to open it. Choose Edit to work on the questions or Results to review responses.
  2. To start a new survey, return to Dashboard and click Create survey.

Tips & Troubleshooting

  • If you can’t find a survey you created, check that you signed in with the email address you used to create it.
  • Give each survey a clear title so you can tell similar surveys apart.

Create your first survey

Overview

The survey builder lets you add questions and choose how people answer them. You can review the survey before publishing a link that people can use to respond.

Prerequisites

  • A SurveyTaps account.
  • A topic and at least one question you want to ask.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. From Dashboard, click Create survey.
  2. Enter a title and, if helpful, add a short description that tells respondents what the survey is about.
  3. Click Add question, choose a question type, and enter your question. If the type uses answer choices, add those too.

[placeholder]

  1. Turn on Required for any question respondents must answer. Repeat steps 3 and 4 for the rest of your questions.
  2. Click Preview to review the survey. Check that the questions appear in the right order and the answer choices are clear.
  3. When you’re ready to collect responses, click Publish, then Copy link. Share the link with the people you want to hear from.

Tips & Troubleshooting

  • Ask one thing per question. For example, ask about delivery speed and packaging quality in two separate questions.
  • Use Required for answers you need. Leave personal or sensitive questions optional where possible.

Preview and test your survey before sharing

Overview

Preview lets you see your survey from a respondent’s point of view. A quick test helps you catch confusing wording, missing answer choices, and layout issues before you share the link.

Prerequisites

  • A survey with at least one question.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Open your survey from Dashboard and click Preview.

[placeholder]

  1. Read the title, instructions, and questions as if you were seeing them for the first time.
  2. Try each question type. Leave a required question blank and use Next or Submit, whichever appears, to check that you see a clear message.
  3. Review the survey on a phone as well as a computer. Check that the text is easy to read and the answer choices are easy to select.
  4. Return to Edit to fix any issues, then open Preview again to check your changes.
  5. When the survey is ready, click Publish, then Copy link. Open the link in a private browser window to check what respondents see before you share it.

Tips & Troubleshooting

  • If you use the published link to test the survey, stop before clicking Submit to keep your test answer out of the results.
  • Ask someone who hasn’t seen the survey to try it. Pay attention to questions they need to read twice.
